OUR STORY

A Dream Turned Into Handmade Treasures

Hi, I'm Ayesha — the heart behind Willow & Oak.

What started as a little hobby, creating thoughtful gifts for the people I love, slowly grew into something much more meaningful. I discovered that the smallest things often carry the biggest emotions.

From choosing beautiful materials to carefully wrapping every order, I put a little piece of myself into everything I create. Every piece of jewelry, every candle, and every personalized gift is made to help someone feel remembered and loved.

Willow & Oak is my little corner of creativity, a place where ordinary moments become special memories, one handmade treasure at a time.

Made slowly, given with love. ♥
